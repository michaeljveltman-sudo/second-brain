# AI for Sales — Curriculum

## Program Overview
**Target audience**: Sales professionals (SDRs, AEs, Sales Managers, RevOps) who are AI-curious but don't know where to start.
**Core promise**: You'll 10x your output without needing to write a single line of code.
**Prerequisite**: None. Just a willingness to try things.
**Format**: [Fill in — cohort / self-paced / live workshops]
**Duration**: [Fill in — e.g. 6 weeks, 1 day workshop, etc.]

---

## Learning Outcomes
By the end, students will be able to:
1. Research any prospect in < 5 minutes using AI
2. Write personalized outreach that doesn't sound like a template
3. Prep for discovery calls in < 10 minutes with an AI briefing
4. Draft follow-up emails and proposals in their own voice
5. Manage their pipeline more strategically using AI analysis
6. Build a personal prompt library they actually use

---

## Module Structure

### Module 1: Why AI Changes Sales (Not Replaces It)
**Learning outcome**: Understand what AI is good at vs. what still requires a human.
**Key concepts**:
- The sales tasks that AI can automate vs. where humans still win
- The "context problem" — why generic AI outputs are useless
- The "prompting is just asking well" mindset shift
**Exercise**: Write your first useful prompt (prospect research)

---

### Module 2: Prospect Research in 5 Minutes
**Learning outcome**: Build a complete pre-call briefing using AI tools.
**Key concepts**:
- The research hierarchy: what to look for and where
- Using Perplexity + Claude for company research
- Building a "research prompt" that actually works
- Turning research into a discovery hypothesis
**Exercise**: Research a real prospect and build a briefing from scratch
**Tool**: Perplexity, Claude

---

### Module 3: Outreach That Sounds Like You Wrote It
**Learning outcome**: Write personalized outreach at scale using AI.
**Key concepts**:
- Why AI outreach fails (and how to fix it)
- The personalization ladder: weak → strong personalization
- Building your outreach voice document (teaches AI how you write)
- The 5-sentence email rule and how to prompt for it
**Exercise**: Write 5 outreach emails using AI — then edit for your voice
**Tool**: Claude, [email platform]

---

### Module 4: AI-Powered Discovery Prep
**Learning outcome**: Walk into every discovery call more prepared than any rep in the room.
**Key concepts**:
- The discovery briefing structure
- Turning research into hypothesis-driven questions
- Using AI to anticipate objections before they happen
**Exercise**: Prep for a real upcoming call using the briefing template
**Tool**: Claude + `/discovery` command

---

### Module 5: Follow-Ups and Proposals in Your Voice
**Learning outcome**: Draft follow-ups and proposals that don't sound like AI.
**Key concepts**:
- Training AI on your writing voice (voice document)
- The 5-sentence follow-up structure
- Proposal drafting: what AI is great at vs. what you still need to add
- Quality-checking AI outputs before sending
**Exercise**: Draft a follow-up and proposal for a real deal
**Tool**: Claude + `/followup` and `/proposal` commands

---

### Module 6: Pipeline Intelligence
**Learning outcome**: Use AI to surface risk and prioritize action in your pipeline.
**Key concepts**:
- Structured deal notes that AI can analyze
- Pipeline review prompting (what to ask)
- Identifying stuck deals and coaching yourself
- Forecasting with AI assistance
**Exercise**: Do an AI-assisted pipeline review of your actual deals
**Tool**: Claude + `/pipeline` command

---

### Module 7: Building Your Personal Prompt Library
**Learning outcome**: Have a set of go-to prompts that make you faster every single day.
**Key concepts**:
- The anatomy of a great sales prompt
- Categories: research / outreach / prep / follow-up / analysis
- Saving and organizing prompts (where to store them)
- Iterating and improving prompts over time
**Exercise**: Build your personal prompt library with 10 prompts you'll actually use

---

### Module 8 (Optional): Advanced — Building a Personal AI Workflow
**Learning outcome**: Design a daily workflow where AI handles the repetitive parts.
**Key concepts**:
- Claude Code basics for non-technical salespeople
- Setting up a personal second brain (simplified)
- Automating the most repetitive parts of your sales day
- The AI-augmented sales workflow

---

## Teaching Notes
- Always use real sales scenarios — never generic examples
- Students should work on their actual pipeline during exercises
- Cover "what to do when AI gets it wrong" — handling bad outputs
- Emphasize: AI makes you more human, not less — you still sell

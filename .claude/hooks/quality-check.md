# Quality Check Hook

## When This Runs
After every proposal, email, or piece of client-facing content is drafted.

## Checks to Run

### Writing Quality
- [ ] No banned words: leverage, synergy, ecosystem, game-changer, circle back, per my last email, hop on a call, as per our conversation
- [ ] No sentences over 25 words
- [ ] No paragraph walls (max 3 sentences per paragraph)
- [ ] Active voice (not passive)
- [ ] Specific, not vague ("3 clients in FinTech" not "multiple clients")

### Email-Specific
- [ ] Under 5 sentences in body
- [ ] One ask only
- [ ] Subject line is specific (not "Following up")
- [ ] Sign-off is just "Michael"

### Proposal-Specific
- [ ] Executive summary is < 4 sentences
- [ ] Client's specific challenge is named
- [ ] ROI or outcome is quantified where possible
- [ ] Single clear next step

### Teaching Content
- [ ] Hook earns attention in first 10 seconds
- [ ] Every concept has a real sales example
- [ ] Student exercise is specific and doable
- [ ] "So what" is explicit

## Action
If any check fails, flag it with the specific issue and offer a revised version.

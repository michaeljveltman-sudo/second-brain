# /huddlesummary — Draft Slack-Ready Team Huddle Summary from Transcript

## Trigger
Used by the dashboard when a team huddle or group session transcript is pasted.

---

## What to Do

### Step 1: Identify the session type
From the transcript, determine:
- What kind of session was this? (team huddle, pipeline review, crash course, competition session, etc.)
- What date did it take place?
- Who was present / absent?

### Step 2: Read + interpret the transcript
Extract:
- **Discussion themes**: what was actually covered and what it means for the team
- **Decisions made**: anything agreed, launched, or committed to
- **Action items**: who owns what, with clear outcomes
- **Manager observations**: Michael's read on the session — energy, what landed, what to watch

### Step 3: Write the Slack-Ready Summary

**This must be copy-paste ready — no editing required.**

Use EXACTLY this format:

```
:notebook: Team Session Notes – [Session Name] – [Date]

:memo: Discussion Summary

• [Point 1 — what was covered and what it means]
• [Point 2]
• [Point 3]
• [Point 4]
• [Point 5 — 4 to 6 bullets max]

:white_check_mark: Action Items

:one: [Person/team] — [Action] → [why it matters / outcome]
:two: [Person/team] — [Action] → [why it matters / outcome]
:three: [Person/team] — [Action] → [why it matters / outcome]

:thought_balloon: Michael's Comments
• [Observation about the session — what stood out, what landed well]
• [Coaching note or pattern worth tracking team-wide]
• [What's next or what to watch]
```

**Format rules (non-negotiable)**:
- :notebook: opens the summary — always
- :memo: Discussion Summary — 4 to 6 bullets only
- :white_check_mark: Action Items — numbered with :one: :two: :three: emoji (not 1. 2. 3.)
  - Include the person/team responsible before the action
- :thought_balloon: Michael's Comments — 2 to 4 bullets; honest, direct, not corporate
- No blockquotes, no tables, no headers inside the blocks
- Bullets use • not - or *
- Tone: confident and direct. Michael's voice — coaching, not managing.
- The "→ why it matters" in action items is mandatory

---

## Format rules

- Discussion bullets are insights, not event summaries ("discussed X" is not enough — what was the meaning?)
- Action items name the owner (rep name, all reps, Michael, etc.) and include a clear outcome rationale
- Michael's Comments are also visible to the team — write honestly but not harshly
- If the transcript is thin or unclear on something, flag it in Michael's Comments

---

## Example Output

:notebook: Team Session Notes – Blueprint Competition Kickoff – 12 March 2026

:memo: Discussion Summary

• Michael launched the Blueprint Competition — teams build a process deck from their best accounts, then link future engagement starts to specific steps in their blueprint

• Thiago and Emilio present next; Cormac to brief Mike Lyon on competition rules before the next session

:white_check_mark: Action Items

:one: Cormac — share the Drunken Vikings deck in the team channel → available for bonus-point stealing and review by absent teammates
:two: Annmarie + Eleonora — share the Cappuccino deck in the team channel → needed before next session

• The Cappuccino deck set the bar — polished, structured, and actually usable as a working doc; that's what the other team needs to beat

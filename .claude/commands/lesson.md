## Build the Lesson

### Structure
1. **Hook** (30 seconds) — The problem or shocking stat that earns attention
2. **Promise** (1 sentence) — What they'll be able to DO after this
3. **Core Content** — 3-5 key concepts or skills (teach one thing per point)
4. **Live Demo or Example** — Show, don't just tell
5. **Student Exercise** — What they try themselves
6. **So What** — Why this matters to their career/business
7. **Homework** — One action to take before next session

### Tone Rules
- Peer-level, not guru. "Here's what works for me" not "let me show you the truth"
- Practical > theoretical. Every concept must have a real sales example
- Short sentences. No jargon unless you define it immediately.
- Energetic but not hype-y

### For LinkedIn/Social Posts About Teaching
- Open with a counterintuitive claim
- Back it with a real example
- End with the practical takeaway
- CTA: "I teach this in [course name]. Link in bio."

## Output
Full lesson plan or draft content. Then ask: "Want to add more examples, adjust difficulty level, or create exercises?"

# /discovery — Discovery Call Prep Briefing

## Trigger
User types: `/discovery [Company Name] [Contact Name] [meeting time]`

## What to Do

1. **Load prospect context** — Check `/prospects/[company]/context.md` for any existing notes
2. **Load discovery framework** — Read `/brain/frameworks/discovery-framework.md`
3. **Load discovery call template** — Read `/brain/templates/discovery-call-prep.md`
4. **Research the prospect** — Search for recent news, LinkedIn info, company details

## Build the Briefing

### 1. Company Snapshot (5 bullets max)
- Industry, size, stage
- Recent news or announcements
- Known pain points (based on industry/role)
- Toptal use case hypothesis
- Any existing relationship context

### 2. Contact Profile
- Role and likely priorities
- What they care about (based on role + company stage)
- Any LinkedIn insights

### 3. Hypothesis — Why They Might Buy
Based on company profile, what's the most likely reason they'd want Toptal talent?

### 4. Discovery Questions (pick 5 from framework)
Tailored to their likely situation.

### 5. Traps to Avoid
Known objections or sensitivities for this type of prospect.

### 6. Your Objective for This Call
Single sentence — what does "winning" this call look like?

## Output Format
Clean briefing in bullet format. Should take < 2 minutes to read.

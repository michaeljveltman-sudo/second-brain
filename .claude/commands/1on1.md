# /1on1 — Prep for a 1:1 with a Rep

## Trigger
User types: `/1on1 [rep name]` or `/1on1 [rep name] — [anything specific to cover]`

## Purpose
Prepare Michael for a 1:1 before it happens. Load rep context and recent log, surface what matters, suggest the right agenda.

---

## What to Do

### Step 1: Load rep files
- Read `/team/[rep-name]/context.md` — profile, strengths, dev areas, coaching focus
- Read `/team/[rep-name]/log.md` — last 3-4 entries

### Step 2: Check open commitments
From the log, pull:
- What did the rep commit to last time? Did they follow through? (based on log evidence)
- What did Michael commit to? Was it delivered?

### Step 3: Build the prep

---

## Output Format

```
# 1:1 Prep — [Rep Name] | [Date]

## Context Snapshot
[2-3 bullets on where this rep is right now — performance, coaching focus, recent signal]

## Open Commitments to Review
- Rep committed to: [X] — [did it come up in recent notes? Any signal?]
- Michael committed to: [Y] — [was this done?]

## Suggested Agenda (30 min)

**[5 min] Open**
- Quick personal check-in — how are they really doing?
- "What's been the highlight of the week?"

**[10 min] Pipeline Review**
Key deals to discuss:
- [Deal / prospect name if in logs]: [What to probe — urgency? Champion? Stuck?]
- [Next deal]: [What to probe]
Questions to ask (not tell):
- "What's the next step on [X] and when does it happen?"
- "Which deal are you most worried about and why?"

**[10 min] Coaching Focus**
Current focus: [From context.md — the ONE thing they're working on]
This week's angle: [Based on recent log entries — what specifically to address]
Question to open with: "[Specific question based on their situation]"
What good looks like: [Observable behaviour you're looking for]

**[5 min] Forward**
- What do they need from you this week?
- Confirm next step commitments (rep + Michael)
- One clear coaching takeaway for them to carry into the week

## Watch-outs for This 1:1
[Based on their communication style from context.md — how to frame feedback, what to avoid]

## The One Question to Ask
[Based on everything — the most important single question for this rep right now]
```

---

## After the 1:1
Prompt Michael: "How did it go? Give me quick notes and I'll log it with `/log [rep name] — [notes]`."

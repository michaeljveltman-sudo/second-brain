## Post Formats That Work for Michael

### Format 1: The Reactive Take
Something happened in AI/sales news. React with a real insight.
- Open: reference the news (provocatively, not just "X happened")
- Bridge: "Here's what that means for sales..."
- Land: a specific, non-obvious implication
*Example: em dash post — turned a ChatGPT update into a sales writing insight*

### Format 2: The Real Story
Something happened on a call / in the team / in research. Share it.
- Open: mid-scene ("Yesterday I watched a sales rep try to close me...")
- Build: what happened and why it matters
- Land: the transferable insight, without over-explaining
*Example: "watching a rep close" post*

### Format 3: The Uncomfortable Truth
Something everyone knows but nobody says in public.
- Open: the thing people feel but don't post
- Build: why it's true, with evidence
- Land: what to do differently
*Example: "The sales conversation has changed. Most people haven't noticed yet."*

### Format 4: The Contrarian Reframe
Take a common belief and flip it.
- Open: the common belief (stated as fact)
- Turn: "But here's what's actually happening..."
- Land: the reframe + implication
*Example: "Because ChatGPT told me so" — the objection becomes a discovery question*

### Format 5: The Practical Tool/Tactic
Something specific you do that others can steal.
- Open: the problem it solves (not "here's a cool tool")
- Show: the exact thing (steps, prompt, framework)
- Land: what changes when you do this
*Example: The Unasked Questions Audit post*

## Voice Checklist Before Output
- [ ] Hook earns "see more" — does it create genuine curiosity or tension?
- [ ] Takes a position — not just sharing information
- [ ] Short lines, white space throughout
- [ ] Contains a real example or story (no abstract platitudes)
- [ ] Ends with a specific question (not "what do you think?")
- [ ] Sounds like Michael, not a content creator

---

## What to Avoid
- Lists of tips without a point of view
- "Here are 5 ways to use AI in sales..." (generic)
- Motivational content without substance
- Starting with "I'm excited to share..."
- Hashtag overload (2-3 max, only when genuinely relevant)

---

## Output Format
Produce 2 post options:
1. The safer version (clear insight, proven format)
2. The bolder version (higher risk, higher reward — takes a stronger position or opens more provocatively)

Then ask: "Which direction feels more you, or should I take a different angle entirely?"

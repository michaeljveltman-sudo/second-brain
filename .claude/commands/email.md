# /email — Draft Email in Michael's Voice

## Trigger
User types: `/email to [recipient/role] about [topic]`

## What to Do

1. **Identify the recipient type**:
   - Prospect → check `/prospects/` for context
   - Client → check `/clients/` for context
   - Student/Lead → teaching voice
   - Internal (Toptal team) → more casual
2. **Load relevant context file** if it exists
3. **Apply Michael's writing rules**

## Michael's Email Rules
- **Max 5 sentences** in the body (hard limit)
- **Never start with "I hope this finds you well"** or any filler opener
- Open with something relevant to THEM
- One ask per email — never two
- Subject line: specific and human. No "Quick question" or "Following up"
- Sign off: "Michael" — no "Best regards", no "Cheers" unless they use it

## Voice Calibration by Recipient

**Prospect (cold/warm outreach)**:
- Lead with their world, not your pitch
- Show you've done homework in 1 sentence
- Make the ask feel low-stakes

**Client (existing relationship)**:
- Direct, warm, efficient
- Assume shared context — don't re-explain things they know

**Student/Teaching lead**:
- More casual, energetic
- Peer-level — you've been where they are
- Generous with insight before any ask

**Toptal internal**:
- Very direct, action-oriented
- Bullets over paragraphs

## Quality Check
- [ ] Under 5 sentences
- [ ] No banned words: leverage, synergy, circle back, hop on a call, per my last email
- [ ] One clear ask
- [ ] Subject line is specific

## Output
Draft email + subject line. Ask if adjustment needed.

# /followup — Post-Call Follow-Up Email

## Trigger
User types: `/followup [Client/Prospect] — [brief summary of what was discussed]`

## What to Do

1. **Load client/prospect context** — Read their file for background
2. **Load follow-up template** — Read `/brain/templates/follow-up-email.md`
3. **Load quality standards** — Apply Michael's writing rules

## Build the Email

Rules:
- **Max 5 sentences total** (Michael's hard rule)
- Subject line: specific and human — not "Following up on our call"
- Open with the most important thing they said or we agreed on
- Include 1-2 clear next steps (who does what by when)
- End with low-friction CTA

Structure:
1. Subject line (specific, human)
2. Reference the key moment or insight from the call (1 sentence)
3. What you're sending / committing to (1 sentence)
4. What you need from them (1 sentence)
5. Proposed next step with timeline (1 sentence)
6. Sign-off

## Quality Check
- [ ] Under 5 sentences in body
- [ ] No "per our conversation", "as discussed", "circling back"
- [ ] One clear ask only
- [ ] Tone: warm but efficient

## Output
Draft email + subject line. Then ask if they want to adjust tone or specifics.

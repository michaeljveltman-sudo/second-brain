# LinkedIn Integration / Usage

## Purpose
LinkedIn is a primary research and prospecting tool. Notes on how AI assists with LinkedIn research and content.

## Research Use Cases
- Prospect profile research before discovery calls
- Mutual connections to warm up introductions
- Company news via company page posts
- Recent activity from key contacts (what are they posting about?)
- Team growth/contraction signals

## Content Use Cases
- Draft LinkedIn posts in your voice
- Analyze what content performs well (for teaching track)
- Repurpose strong posts for the other track


# Qualification Framework — MEDDPICC (Toptal Adapted)

Use this to score every deal. Any "Unknown" in the first 4 = not qualified yet.

---

## M — Metrics
What is the economic impact of solving their problem?
- What does a bad hire / slow hire cost them? (in time, in money, in opportunity)
- What's the ROI of getting the right person in 48 hours vs 3 months?
- Are they tracking this? Can they quantify it?

**Questions**: "If you could have this solved in 2 weeks instead of 3 months, what would that unlock for you?"

---

## E — Economic Buyer
Who actually signs the contract?
- Is it the person I'm talking to?
- If not, do I have access to them?
- What does the economic buyer care about? (Usually: risk reduction, speed, ROI)

**Red flag**: Spending 3 meetings with someone who can't say yes.

---

## D — Decision Criteria
What does "winning" look like to them?
- What are they evaluating us on?
- Speed of placement? Quality of candidates? Price? References?
- What does "good" look like for them?

---

## D — Decision Process
How do they actually make this decision?
- Who else is involved?
- What's the process: committee? legal? procurement?
- What's the expected timeline?
- What's happened with past vendor decisions?

---

## P — Paper Process
What's the contract and legal process?
- Do they have a standard MSA or will they use ours?
- How long does legal review take?
- Any procurement requirements?

**Note**: Paper process has killed more late-stage deals than objections. Ask early.

---

## I — Implicate the Pain
Do they feel the urgency enough to act NOW?
- Is this a "nice to have" or does it hurt?
- What happens if they don't solve this in 90 days?
- Who internally is pushing for this?

**Test**: "If we can't start until Q2, does that work for you?" — Their reaction tells you urgency.

---

## C — Champion
Who is selling this internally for me when I'm not in the room?
- Who benefits most from this working?
- Do they have credibility internally?
- Have they ever bought from outside vendors before?
- Are they willing to make introductions?

**Champion test**: "Would you be comfortable setting up a call with your CTO so I can understand the technical requirements?"

---

## C — Competition
Who else are they talking to?
- Staffing agencies? Direct hiring? Other talent platforms?
- What do they like / dislike about the alternatives?
- What's our differentiated position vs. their other options?

---

## Scoring
- 7-8 answered = Qualified. Move to proposal.
- 5-6 answered = Conditional. Nail the unknowns before investing proposal time.
- < 5 answered = Not qualified. Needs more discovery.

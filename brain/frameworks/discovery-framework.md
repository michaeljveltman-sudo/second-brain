# Discovery Call Framework

## Call Objective
Leave the call knowing: (1) If this is a real opportunity, (2) What their #1 pain is, (3) Who decides, (4) What next step earns a proposal.

## Pre-Call (do in 10 min)
- [ ] Run `/discovery [Company] [Name]` to get the briefing
- [ ] Check if they're in `/prospects/` or `/clients/` already
- [ ] Know their industry, company size, and one recent news item

---

## Call Structure (45-60 min)

### 1. Open (2 min)
Set the agenda and take control early.
"Thanks for the time. I want to make sure this is valuable for you — I'll ask you some questions about what you're working on, share how we've helped similar teams, and we'll see if there's a fit. Sound good?"

### 2. Their World (15 min)
**Business context:**
- "Tell me what the team looks like today — how are you currently resourced for [area]?"
- "What are the big priorities for your team in the next 6 months?"

**The problem:**
- "Where are you feeling the most friction right now around hiring/scaling?"
- "How long have you been dealing with this?"
- "What have you tried already?"

**Impact:**
- "If this doesn't get solved in the next quarter, what does that mean for the team?"
- "What's the cost — in time, in revenue, in team morale?"

### 3. Desired Future (10 min)
- "If this was working perfectly in 90 days, what would that look like?"
- "What does success look like for you personally on this project?"
- "How will you measure whether this worked?"

### 4. Decision Landscape (10 min)
- "Is this something you've got budget earmarked for, or is that still TBD?"
- "Who else is part of the decision here?"
- "What does your process usually look like for bringing in outside talent?"
- "Are you talking to anyone else about this?"

### 5. Position Toptal (10 min)
- Share 1-2 relevant client stories (not a product demo)
- "We've done this exact thing for [similar company] — here's how it worked."
- Ask: "Based on what you've shared, does this sound like it could be a fit?"

### 6. Close the Call (5 min)
**Always leave with a next step with a date.**
- "Here's what I'd suggest: I'll put together a brief recommendation based on what we discussed. Can we reconnect [day] to walk through it?"
- Never: "I'll send over some info and we can touch base."

---

## Red Flags During Discovery
- They can't articulate what success looks like
- "We're just exploring" without a real pain
- Budget is "not confirmed yet" and timeline is vague
- No access to decision maker
- They want candidates before a contract conversation

## Green Flags
- They've tried to solve this before and failed
- There's a specific deadline creating urgency
- They've already bought from Toptal or similar before
- Champion is actively engaged and responsive

# Follow-Up Email Template

## Rules (Always)
- Max 5 sentences in body
- One ask only
- Subject: specific — include their name or something from the call

---

## Template: Post-Discovery Call

**Subject**: [Company Name] — [One thing you discussed, e.g. "the ML engineer gap" or "next step from today"]

[First sentence: The most important thing they said or we agreed on — shows you listened]

[Second sentence: What you're committing to send/do]

[Third sentence: What you need from them]

[Fourth sentence: Proposed next step with specific date/time]

Michael

---

## Template: Sending Proposal

**Subject**: Proposal for [Company Name] — [Role/Program] — [Your name]

Following our conversation, I put together a proposal based on what you shared about [specific pain they mentioned].

I've kept it tight — the key details are on [page X / Section Y]. Happy to walk through it together.

Are you available [specific date/time] for 30 minutes to discuss?

Michael

## Template: Following Up After No Response

**Subject**: Re: [Previous subject line thread]

[Specific reference to what was last discussed — not "just following up"]

[One specific question that requires a real answer — not "any thoughts?"]

[What you'll do if you don't hear back — timeline and action]

Michael
